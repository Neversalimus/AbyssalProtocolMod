

## T5 passive turret module budget

T5 turret modules should include several passive roles, not only Crown Aegis Matrix.

| Module | Slot | Tier | Role | Target budget |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Crown Aegis Matrix | Passive | 5 | shield survival | one Aegis module per turret; no stacking |
| Dominion Verdict Lens | Passive | 5 | boss/elite targeting + range | +6 range, -0.35 miss radius, strong boss/construct/mech/shield priority, 1.05 cooldown, +520 W |
| Crown Overpressure Choir | Passive | 5 | firing cadence overclock | 0.86 cooldown, -2.5 range, +0.15 miss radius, 1.06 incoming damage, +760 W |
| Sepulcher Fail-Safe Mantle | Passive | 5 | chassis survival/recovery | 0.76 incoming damage, +0.28 idle cooldown recovery, -2 min range, 1.07 cooldown, +700 W |

Do not add a second T5 Aegis passive unless the turret runtime is intentionally changed; current turret safety rules enforce one shield module and prevent Aegis stacking.
